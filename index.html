About

How incentives and institutions shape behaviour, and how to measure it credibly with data: causal inference, experimental design, and machine learning for economic questions.

Yan-Ting Chen is a senior in Economics at National Taiwan University, graduating in December 2026. He is a Research Assistant in the TASSEL Lab under Prof. Joseph Tao-yi Wang, where he analyses a migrant–local sender–receiver game experiment on how language structure forms and is learned. Before that, he worked as a Digital Finance × AI Intern at Cathay United Bank, a Technology Consultant Intern at EY, and a Software Engineer Intern at Flexsoft.
